<h2><a href="https://leetcode.com/problems/largest-rectangle-in-histogram">84. Largest Rectangle in Histogram</a></h2><h3>Hard</h3><hr><p>Given the bar heights of a histogram (each bar 1 wide), return the area of the largest rectangle that fits inside it.</p>
<p><strong class="example">Example 1:</strong></p>
<pre>
<strong>Input:</strong> heights = [2,1,5,6,2,3]
<strong>Output:</strong> 10
<strong>Explanation:</strong> Bars 5 and 6 give 5 * 2 = 10.
</pre>
<p><strong class="example">Example 2:</strong></p>
<pre>
<strong>Input:</strong> heights = [2,4]
<strong>Output:</strong> 4
</pre>
<p><strong>Constraints:</strong> <code>1 &lt;= heights.length &lt;= 10<sup>5</sup></code>, <code>0 &lt;= heights[i] &lt;= 10<sup>4</sup></code>.</p>
