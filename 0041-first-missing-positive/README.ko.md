<pre>

</pre>

<pre>
<strong>입력:</strong> nums = [3,4,-1,1]
<strong>출력:</strong> 2
</pre>
<p><strong class="example">예시 3:</strong></p>
<pre>
<strong>입력:</strong> nums = [7,8,9,11,12]
<strong>출력:</strong> 1
</pre>
<p><strong>제약 조건:</strong> <code>1 &lt;= nums.length &lt;= 10<sup>5</sup></code>.</p>
