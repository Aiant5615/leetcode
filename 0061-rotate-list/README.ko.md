<h2><a href="https://leetcode.com/problems/rotate-list">61. Rotate List</a></h2><h3>Medium</h3><hr><p>연결 리스트를 오른쪽으로 <code>k</code>칸 회전하고 새 head를 반환하세요.</p>
<p><strong class="example">예시 1:</strong></p>
<pre>
<strong>입력:</strong> head = [1,2,3,4,5], k = 2
<strong>출력:</strong> [4,5,1,2,3]
</pre>
<p><strong class="example">예시 2:</strong></p>
<pre>
<strong>입력:</strong> head = [0,1,2], k = 4
<strong>출력:</strong> [2,0,1]
</pre>
<p><strong>제약 조건:</strong> 노드 수 0~500, <code>0 &lt;= k &lt;= 2 * 10<sup>9</sup></code>.</p>
