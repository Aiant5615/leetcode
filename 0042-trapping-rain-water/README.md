<h2><a href="https://leetcode.com/problems/trapping-rain-water">42. Trapping Rain Water</a></h2><h3>Hard</h3><hr><p>Given non-negative bar heights <code>height</code> (each bar 1 wide), compute how much rain water is trapped between the bars.</p>
<p><strong class="example">Example 1:</strong></p>
<pre>
<strong>Input:</strong> height = [0,1,0,2,1,0,1,3,2,1,2,1]
<strong>Output:</strong> 6
</pre>
<p><strong class="example">Example 2:</strong></p>
<pre>
<strong>Input:</strong> height = [4,2,0,3,2,5]
<strong>Output:</strong> 9
</pre>
<p><strong>Constraints:</strong> <code>1 &lt;= n &lt;= 2 * 10<sup>4</sup></code>, <code>0 &lt;= height[i] &lt;= 10<sup>5</sup></code>.</p>
